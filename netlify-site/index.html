About

Declan Treanor helps high performers build sustainable habits across mind, body, and soul. Drawing on psychology, sport, and years of coaching experience, Performance Treanor supports people who want better energy, focus, and wellbeing — without gimmicks.

This site is the new home for Performance Treanor online. When performancetreanor.com goes live, it will point here — same content, your domain.

Daily Diesel app

The Daily Diesel app brings Declan’s course modules, videos, workbooks, and resources to your phone. Track progress, build streaks, and work through holistic performance topics at your own pace.

  • Video modules hosted on Vimeo
  • Digital and printable workbooks
  • Articles and podcasts in Resources

Articles & podcasts

Long-form articles and podcast episodes are read and played inside the Daily Diesel app — streamed over the internet, not bundled into the download, so the app stays lightweight.

Media can be hosted here on Netlify for now. When you are ready, upload converted MP3 files to /media/podcasts/ and update the app to use this site as mediaBaseUrl instead of the legacy WordPress URLs.

Tip: Convert large WAV podcast files to MP3 before uploading — much faster for listeners on mobile data.
